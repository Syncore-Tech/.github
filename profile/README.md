# Syncore JSC: AI integration & AI agents for the systems you already run

**Syncore** is a software company in Vietnam that brings AI, AI agents and automation into a company's existing ERP, CRM and data. We build on the systems that already make you money instead of rebuilding from scratch.

## What we do

| Service | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **AI agent development** | Agents that do real work inside your systems: look up orders, draft quotes, triage requests, update the CRM. Tool use (function calling, MCP), role-based access, human approval and a full audit trail. |
| **AI integration for existing systems** | RAG assistants that answer from your own documents and data. Connectors for ERP, CRM, Google Workspace and Lark. Model chosen by evidence: Claude, GPT or open source. |
| **Legacy system modernization** | Take over unfinished or aging software and upgrade it piece by piece, without stopping the business that runs on it. Compliant with Vietnam's Decree 13/2023 on personal data. |
| **Workflow automation & data sync** | Bring scattered data into one trusted source and automate data entry, reconciliation and reporting. |

## How we work: Sync → Standardize → Extend

1. **Sync.** Connect the systems you already run through one shared core layer. Nothing that works gets replaced.
2. **Standardize.** Clean and unify data and workflows, the foundation AI needs to give correct answers.
3. **Extend.** Add AI agents, document assistants and automation where they create value.

You see it working before you sign the quote.

## Selected work

- **Hospital information system for cleft lip and palate care** (MSC, Van Lang University). We took over an unfinished HIS and completed it: digital clinical forms, treatment rules by age band and specialty, and a pilot on synthetic data before any real patient data.
- **AI sales-coaching platform for real estate agents** (Horizon Realty, Austin, TX). An AI plays the prospect so agents can rehearse cold calls and objections. Integrated with the Follow Up Boss CRM, shipped as a web app and iOS PWA.
- **Products we helped design and build:** ELIBOT (AI desktop companion), AiriGo (robotics), Epic Ring (AI accessories), Raybit (health & wellness).
